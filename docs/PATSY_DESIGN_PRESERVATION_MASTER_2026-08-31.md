# 1. PATSY IS PATSY — CHARACTER IDENTITY LOCK

## 1.1 Canonical app Patsy

The main app companion is **MASTER APP PATSY / MASTER_APP_PATSY_POSE_SHEET**.

She is:
- a small grey Maltipoo;
- realistic/high-grade, not a generic doodle/poodle;
- grey shaggy/wavy coat;
- clearly visible large white chest;
- white muzzle;
- very dark/almost-black expressive eyes with only subtle brown depth in light;
- longer scruffy/wavy hair on top of the head;
- eyebrow fur stays grey, not white;
- darker-grey ears;
- ear fur straighter/silkier and less fluffy than body fur;
- drop ears begin at the side of the skull;
- ears are narrow/thin almost all the way down;
- slight lift/outward movement at the ear base is allowed, then the ear drops naturally;
- lower ear may have a subtle twist;
- full/fluffy expressive tail;
- compact natural paws;
- approved master body/paw/tail proportions;
- expressive face, not one permanent mascot smile.

### Signature expressions / behaviour
Very happy, extremely excited/tongue out, cheeky, curious/head tilt, judgy/side-eye, thinking, listening, concerned, calm, playful, alert, resting.

### Absolute rejection list
Do not replace app Patsy with:
- a generic Maltipoo/doodle;
- a cartoon main-app Patsy;
- a fox/cat/other animal;
- a square/rectangular dog photo;
- a visible image-card/background;
- a static PNG pose swap system;
- GIFs;
- sprite sheets;
- a video loop;
- abrupt full-picture expression changes.

Real Patsy photographs remain anatomy/identity references for face, ears, coat, eyes and expression.
