# Patsy App — PawMoji / Codex Handoff
Updated: 2026-08-29

## Global app branding rules
- NEVER use DontWorryBePatsy as the main Patsy App branding.
- Official app identity remains the approved white Patsy logo.
- Small, occasional DontWorryBePatsy Easter eggs ARE approved inside PawMoji/sticker props or packaging (for example the donut box). Keep them subtle and infrequent; never turn them into primary branding.
- Use a gender-neutral, varied colour system across PawMojis: yellow, green, red, blue, purple, orange, black/charcoal, teal and rainbow accents. Pink is allowed occasionally but must not dominate the whole set.

## PawMoji production style
- PawMoji Patsy is deliberately separate from the realistic animated App Patsy.
- Cute polished 3D/cartoon/sticker appearance.
- Fluffy grey/cream Patsy, darker floppy ears, huge glossy brown eyes, black nose, expressive face.
- Emoji assets should be single isolated cutouts with transparent backgrounds.
- Background/scene-heavy variants can be treated as stickers.
- Props/symbols may be integrated into the cutout.
- Hats/sunglasses should be occasional rather than appearing on every PawMoji.

## Remember Me paw system
- Remembered/locked content uses Patsy’s actual paw outline.
- Open state: thin rainbow outline.
- Saved/locked state: fully filled bright neon rainbow paw.
- On activation: ~1 second glow/pulse plus soft bubble-like “bloop”; optional haptic/sound setting.
- Filled neon rainbow paw is reserved for the remembered/locked state and should remain visually distinct from ordinary PawMoji decoration.

## Separation from realistic App Patsy
- Realistic App Patsy remains the animated in-app assistant/companion and must not be replaced by PawMoji Patsy.
- Only one realistic App Patsy should appear on an app screen at once.
- PawMojis are emoji/sticker assets for communication and UI use.
