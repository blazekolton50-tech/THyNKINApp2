# Animated Patsy Character Reference

These five images were recovered from the ChatGPT task **Animated Patsy Character** and are bundled as UI, positioning and motion-direction references:

- `5421.png`
- `5422.png`
- `5423.png`

They establish that Patsy must appear directly over the black/charcoal interface without a square, move between controls, point/wave with her own paw, guide contextual actions, shrink/rest and expand smoothly, and remain continuously animated while talking and reacting.

They are not the source for Patsy's exact physical identity. The real-photo identity authority remains in:

- `app/src/main/assets/references/latest/real_patsy_face_identity_REFERENCE_ONLY.png`
- `app/src/main/assets/references/latest/real_patsy_ears_tail_REFERENCE_ONLY_1.png`
- `app/src/main/assets/references/latest/real_patsy_ears_tail_REFERENCE_ONLY_2.png`

Those photos confirm the darker, straighter low-hanging ear hair, grey/white coat, white muzzle and chest, dark eyes, black nose, natural body proportions, head tilt and distinctive dark curved tail. They are reference-only and must not be rendered as the in-app Patsy character.

The release character must be an authored transparent Rive/Live2D-quality rig or equivalent continuous skeletal/mesh animation. Static screenshots, GIF-like pose swapping and square collage assets do not satisfy the requirement.
